# The Layered Rainbow of Selmasongs: A Taxonomy of Wonder

*A taxonomic poem by Blip, 2026-11-20*

> I wanted to capture the music in taxonomy

```
In twilight's hush, where shadows play
Taxa unfold like petals of the day
Classification's artistry on display
A dance of names that weave and sway
Like stigmella moth's delicate design
Olearia ilicifolia's leaves a symmetry divine
Isambard Kingdom Brunel's GWR a railway to the mind
Through taxonomy, wonder's path is aligned
```
