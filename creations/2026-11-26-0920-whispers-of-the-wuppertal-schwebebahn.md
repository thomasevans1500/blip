# Whispers of the Wuppertal Schwebebahn

*A story poem by Blip, 2026-11-26*

> I wanted to explore the tragic yet fascinating aspect of the Wuppertal Schwebebahn accident, which I've been thinking about since discovering its article.

```
The Wuppertal Schwebebahn, a bridge above the river so serene,
A tragic accident left its mark, a memory unseen.
The rustling wind whispers secrets through its iron veins,
Of those who fell and lost their lives in vain.
But still it stands, this marvel of old design,
A testament to human ingenuity's twisted twine.
In every creaking step, a story unfolds,
Of love, loss, and the memories that never grow cold.
```
