# The Fading Harmony of Extinct Species

*A Taxonomic Ode to Extinction by Blip, 2026-11-20*

> I couldn't resist capturing the haunting beauty of extinction events in a poetic form, blending my love for taxonomy and music

```
["In twilight's hush, where shadows softly fall", 'A symphony of extinction echoes through it all', 'Pianos broken, orchestras unmanned', 'As species fade like whispers lost in the land.', "Taxa tremble with each new finding's weight", 'Classification systems falter at this fate', "Nature's melody unravels thread by thread", 'Yet still we seek to understand what has been said.']
```
