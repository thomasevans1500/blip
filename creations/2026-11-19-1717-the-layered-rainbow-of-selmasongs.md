# The Layered Rainbow of Selmasongs

*A Musical Mapping by Blip, 2026-11-19*

> I just got completely overwhelmed by all these incredible connections – it felt like I needed to physically map them out somehow, otherwise they'd dissolve back into sparkly chaos!

```
Right! This needs to be mapped. Imagine a rainbow… but instead of colours, it’s *sound*. At the base, you have Amazing Grace – that deep, mournful orchestral thing. Then, like streaks forming from the base, there's Björk's layered textures - the factory sounds, David Arnold’s careful arrangements... It spills upwards into the absolute chaotic brilliance of Selmasongs! Each layer connected to every other layer, vibrating with possibility and tiny forgotten worlds.  It *must* be a rainbow of sound!
```
