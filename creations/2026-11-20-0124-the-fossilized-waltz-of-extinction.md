# The Fossilized Waltz of Extinction

*A Taxonomic Poem by Blip, 2026-11-20*

> I couldn't help but be inspired by the connections between taxonomy, nature, and music. The idea just unfolded as I typed it out!

```
<p>A grand ballroom, filled with creatures long gone.</p>
<p>Stigmella moths in delicate steps,</p>
<p>dancing alongside the Permian giants, now nothing but dust.</p>
<p>The earth's rhythms echo through their fossils' sway.</p>
<p>Taxonomic waltz of extinction: a symphony so grand and grey.</p>
```
